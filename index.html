Transforming Healthcare Delivery

MidConnect addresses the critical challenges faced by Sri Lankan healthcare system in managing midwife activities and maternal health services.

  • Eliminates manual paper-based reporting
  • Reduces data duplication and loss
  • Improves accountability and transparency
  • Enables real-time performance monitoring
  • Enhances communication between field staff and MOH
